## 1) Anthropic: an unreleased model built five exploit chains against major software (AI-native; lab primary, single source)

**What changed:** Anthropic's Frontier Red Team [post](https://www.anthropic.com/research/discovering-vulnerabilities-with-claude) (dated Oct 27; page modified Tue 2:15 PM) says it used "an unreleased, internal research model" that, "after initial prompting," wrote a one-click Chrome sandbox escape, a Safari same-origin bypass, a Pixel 9 lock-screen data pull, a Windows kernel QUIC remote crash (CVE-2026-33096) and a zero-click Linux root (CVE-2026-98432). Anthropic says Google assigned and patched "62 CVEs … as of Chrome 156," including 13 sandbox escapes.

**Limits:** self-reported; unnamed model, human-prompted. Vendor counts are Anthropic's, not checked against Chrome or MSRC notes. **Action:** keep browsers, Android, Windows and kernels current; assume faster exploit development for disclosed bugs.

## 2) Follow-up (Oct 22): LiteLLM's embargoed advisory is out: CVSS 10, Enterprise proxies (CVE main 1 of 3; AI gateway)

**What changed:** [GHSA-w4c7-34wj-hqm8](https://github.com/BerriAI/litellm/security/advisories/GHSA-w4c7-34wj-hqm8) (Tue 4:33 PM): "Unauthenticated file read leaks master key on Enterprise proxy," rated critical, CVSS 3.1 10.0. Fixed in 1.103.10, 1.104.8, 1.105.6 and 1.106.5. LiteLLM's [blog](https://docs.litellm.ai/blog/litellm-private-registry) (dated Oct 27) says it shipped the fix to enterprise customers through a temporary private registry before disclosure: "Anyone can point a model at our patch diffs."

**Limits:** no CVE ID yet; no exploitation reported; Enterprise-licensed proxies only; separate from the Oct 21 package compromise. **Action:** upgrade, then rotate the master key and every provider key the proxy holds.

## 3) Follow-up (Oct 27): Node2Shell is on KEV, with in-the-wild attempts logged (CVE main 2 of 3; exploited)

**What changed:** CISA's [KEV](https://www.cisa.gov/known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog) (v2026.10.27, Tue 3:00 PM) added Brotli CVE-2026-111497, due **Nov 10**, "based on evidence of active [exploitation](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/10/27/cisa-adds-two-known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog)." **This corrects the Oct 27 edition's "no exploitation reported; not on KEV."** [Previdian](https://previdian.com/CVE-2026-111497) logged one "confirmed" attempt Oct 27. Per [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/cisa-warns-of-node2shell-exploitation-as-honeypots-log-attack-attempts/) (Wed 4:37 AM), Previdian says that "does not mean the attempt succeeded," and Broadcom says VMware SDDC Manager is affected, with patches pending.

**Limits:** [ELLIO](https://ellio.tech/en/blog/node2shell-cve-2026-111497-rce-on-stock-nodejs-builds/) says the in-the-wild exploit it saw Oct 26 (pre-window) "scored zero hits in 50 attempts." No confirmed successful compromise; Broadcom advisory not fetched. **Action:** patch Node.js/Brotli. Until then, block Brotli-compressed request bodies at the proxy. Failed attempts crash workers, so watch for restart loops.

## 4) Missed Oct 27 (primary dated Mon Oct 26; in-window press Wed): Dream says a custom PHP2Shell chain rooted firewalls (CVE main 3 of 3; exploited; AI-assisted per vendor)

**What changed:** [Dream](https://dreamgroup.com/blog/chained-php2shell-exploit-roots-25-servers) says an actor chained a custom PHP2Shell exploit with the Fragnesia kernel LPE (CVE-2026-46300) for unauthenticated root on one unnamed vendor's firewalls. Dream gives two counts: "rooted 25 servers" across the US, Japan, Taiwan and South Korea, and "24 firewalls" with root webshells. Two servers at a US government-affiliated target saw follow-on AD activity. Recovered tooling included Claude Code and DeepSeek/Kimi-backed "aiscan"; Dream has "no direct evidence" AI wrote the exploit.

**Timing:** Dream's post is headed Oct 26 (page modified Tue 1:02 PM), so it likely predates this window. In-window anchor: [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/china-linked-hackers-exploit-php2shell-to-root-firewalls/) (Wed 8:00 AM). **Limits:** single vendor; the China link is Dream's assessment, no actor named; firewall vendor withheld. KEV due date for CVE-2026-109618/-109619 is **Thu Oct 29**; CVE-2026-115560 is still not on KEV. **Action:** ask firewall vendors whether management planes run PHP, and patch.

## 5) 123 Reg calls a Sept 18 breach "AI-driven," suspects China nexus (AI-native; victim statement via press, single source)

**What changed:** 123 Reg told [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/cyber-crime/2026/10/28/123-reg-discloses-ai-cyberattack-on-customer-data-suspects-china-nexus/5305936) (Wed 7:45 AM) the intrusion was "the first known AI-driven cyberattack against our company." It cites attacker infrastructure in China and Singapore and a mirror of "anthropic_skills," and suspects the same group behind CrowdStrike CSA-261162's ARTEX attacks on South Korean banks. Exposed: names, phones, VAT numbers, account data.

**Limits:** the attribution is 123 Reg's own; no IoCs; CrowdStrike hasn't commented; no 123 Reg notice was found. **Action:** expect registrar-themed phishing; turn on 2SV.
