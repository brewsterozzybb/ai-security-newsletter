## 1) Google — Gemini 4 Argon Fairwind cyber-defender rollout

## 3) Laravel AI SDK — SSRF via client-supplied file URLs (GHSA-6qhr-3g93-pxhw)

Laravel **GHSA-6qhr-3g93-pxhw** on **`laravel/ai`** (**`published_at=2026-09-30T16:34:18Z`**; Medium, CVSS **5.3**): Vercel AI SDK adapter and AG-UI adapter accept client file-part URLs and fetch them server-side **without validation** → SSRF to metadata/localhost/private nets; response body passed to the model as a file attachment.
