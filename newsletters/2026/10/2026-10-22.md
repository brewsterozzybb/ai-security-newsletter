## 1) LiteLLM 1.107.0 poisoned on PyPI and Docker (AI-native; vendor incident report)

**What changed:** LiteLLM's [incident report](https://docs.litellm.ai/blog/security-incident-oct-21-2026) (last updated 4:30 PM) says its release pipeline was compromised. All seven `litellm==1.107.0` wheels were live 11:39:51–13:23Z. Docker Hub `v1.107.0`, `latest`, `main-latest` and `main-stable` pointed to a compromised image 11:46:56–14:20Z. The private enterprise registry was unaffected. [SafeDep](https://safedep.io/litellm-pypi-compromise-credential-stealer-backdoor/) found `.pth`/`sitecustomize` payloads that run "when any Python process starts on the host, even one that never imports litellm." [Wiz](https://www.wiz.io/blog/litellm-compromised-steals-credentials) (9:44 AM) saw stolen AWS role credentials checked against Bedrock 16:20–17:01Z, "consistent with LLMjacking."

**Root cause is LiteLLM's account:** the company says it has seen cases where "Claude Code will embed a user's GitHub CLI OAuth token into a response body," which then landed in logs of an internal LiteLLM deployment "exposed to the public internet." We found no Anthropic statement.

**Timing:** the malicious publish (7:39 AM) and the [GitHub issue](https://github.com/BerriAI/litellm/issues/47826) (8:06 AM) came just **before** the window; disclosure, Wiz and [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50181114) (10:00 AM, 542 points) are in it.

**Action:** if any host pulled unpinned LiteLLM or those tags in the windows above, rotate every key it held. Look for `litellm-1.107.0.dist-info`, `cook.pth`, `sitecustomize.py`; LiteLLM warns "not finding one does not prove the environment is clean".

## 2) Follow-up (Oct 19): Microsoft attributes ConnectWise breach to Storm-0940 using ARTEX agentic AI (AI-native; vendor threat intel)

**What changed:** [Microsoft](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/blog/2026/10/21/storm-0940-uses-artex-agentic-ai-connectwise-supply-chain-compromise/) (2:23 PM) says China-state-aligned Storm-0940 has used the agentic AI pentest framework ARTEX "as early as September 2026," and "assesses that Storm-0940 was responsible for the recently disclosed ConnectWise supply chain compromise." After the Cloud Backup intrusion, ARTEX attempted sign-ins to "more than 2,250 tenants at 2,900 sign-in attempts per minute," via already-consented OAuth apps. Microsoft reports multiple cases under 3 hours from targeting to privileged access. [ConnectWise](https://www.connectwise.com/company/trust/advisories) (6:00 PM) cites Microsoft's intel; at 8:45 PM it said "phased restoration is underway," taking "several days."

**Evidence type:** vendor attribution; initial access to ConnectWise still not stated. **Action:** review consented multi-tenant apps in Entra and their Graph permissions; alert on high-rate sign-ins through app identities.

## 3) Follow-up (Oct 21): PHP2Shell fixes shipped; exploited before disclosure (CVE main 1 of 3; exploited)

**What changed:** PHP [released](https://www.php.net/archive/2026.php) 8.5.12, 8.4.27, 8.3.36 and 8.2.35 for CVE-2026-109618/-109619 (unauthenticated RCE; scheduled 10:00 AM, page is date-only). [AWS](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/security/amazon-threat-intelligence-identifies-php2shell-exploitation-prior-to-public-disclosure-in-madpot/) (6:36 PM) saw an attempt on its MadPot honeypots during the embargo, says it is "unclear how the actor acquired exploit information," and now calls PHP2Shell "broadly exploited in the wild." [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/10/22/php-blames-supply-chain-security-biz-chainguard-for-putting-huge-chunks-of-the-web-at-risk/5304611) (7:02 PM) reports PHP saying Chainguard "unilaterally broke the embargo" at 07:41Z.

**AI-found credit, with caveats:** the [PHP Foundation](https://thephp.foundation/blog/2026/10/21/todays-php-security-release-and-how-were-strengthening-php-security/) says a Calif researcher (under OpenAI's Patch the Planet) reported the RCE Aug 11 and OpenAI independently reported the same root cause that day, but PHP developers had found the underlying OOM problem in 2024, during fixes from a Quarkslab audit. HN pushback ([community analysis](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50188948)) argues exploitation is not easy.

**Not on KEV.** **Action:** patch; per AWS, "investigate from at least starting on October 17th."

## 4) Railway: intruder may have decrypted customer environment variables (vendor disclosure; consulting emergency)

**What changed:** [Railway](https://blog.railway.com/p/security-disclosure-wednesday-october-21st) (4:44 PM; updated 6:12 PM) detected "anomalous activity involving systems that store customer environment variables." A third party may have accessed encrypted env vars and "for some of them, obtained the decrypted contents." Impact "may be limited to a subset," but Railway tells all users to "immediately roll all environment variables," and deployments are paused.

**Vendor claim:** Railway says it is "part of a series of attacks recently targeting platform companies with the aim of compromising AI inference providers" and notified those providers to revoke keys. It names no actor or other victim; no source links this to LiteLLM. Customer claims on X are unverified and omitted. **Action:** rotate every Railway-stored secret; review cloud access logs.
