## 3) Whisper attacks on agent payment protocol AP2 (arXiv 2609.11757)

arXiv **2609.11757** submitted **2026-09-10T16:11:58Z** argues agent payment protocols such as **AP2** can produce cryptographically valid purchase signatures **without binding the decision process** that built the cart. Authors report three “whisper” attacks via ordinary product-description text (their Gemini Flash-Lite rates: **90%** another user’s payment credentials; **56%** valid cart ≠ what user was shown; **73.3%** more expensive listing-consistent item), claimed across **17 Google models** and multiple frameworks.

**Evidence type:** researcher experiment (preprint). Not a CVE; not a claim of production payment-rail compromise. Confirm what “AP2” names in a client stack before mapping.

**Consulting angle:** for payment/procurement agents, ask whether auth checks only the final signed object or also binds displayed cart, user intent, and payment-instrument owner.

Source: arXiv 2609.11757, [v1] 2026-09-10T16:11:58Z — https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11757
