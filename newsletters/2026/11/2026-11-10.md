## 1) Anthropic launches Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 (lab/product)
**What changed:** Anthropic (dated Nov 9, 2026; no time on page; HN Mon 1:01 PM EST, 697 points; X Mon 1:57 PM) says Fable and Mythos 5.2 are the same model. Fable ships with cyber and biology safeguards and is broadly available; Mythos is gated through expanded verification programs. Official scores: CyScenarioBench **76.3%** (Mythos 5.2) vs **61.7%** Mythos 5.1 and **61%** GPT-6 Cyber; CVE Rediscovery **89.6%** (October run of **1,829** CVEs) vs **81.3%** for Opus 5.5 "without safeguards" and **78.3%** for Mythos 5.1. Safeguards are "as robust" as 5.1's "but produce fewer unnecessary blocks," with new evaluations of behaviors "like sandbox escapes."
**Limits:** All figures are Anthropic's. CyScenarioBench footnotes that each lab used unique tasks. HN commenters questioned the CVE Rediscovery chart (contamination; comparison choices).
**Action:** Inventory Mythos vs Fable access; don't treat fewer blocks as a hard control.

## 2) Fireworks expands the October incident; Notion AI transcripts were in the dataset (official)
**What changed:** Fireworks (last updated Nov 9, 2026; no time on page) now says the October incident reached account and billing information (card numbers "not stored" and "not impacted") and, for a limited set of accounts, secrets, datasets, and incomplete model weights. A third-party cloud host of Fireworks R&D machines was breached Oct 23; on Oct 24 the actor reached Fireworks development machines. Fireworks ties the actor to financially motivated LLMJacking and says Inference, Training SDK, and Nexus (ZDR by default) were not impacted. Notion (no time on the page): **<1%** of workspaces may be affected; some **unattributable** Insights transcripts were in a dataset confirmed Nov 8 (Fireworks notified Notion Nov 6). Notion says it disconnected and deleted Fireworks data as of Nov 3.
**Limits:** Investigation ongoing. "Unattributable" is Notion's wording. Cloud provider unnamed.
**Action:** Notion owners: use the desktop/web "Security incident review" pane and rotate secrets pasted into Notion AI. Inventory batch/dataset jobs vs ZDR inference.

## 3) DeepMind's AlphaProof Code verifies key properties of Signal's implementation (lab)
**What changed:** Google DeepMind (dated Nov 10, 2026; no time on the visible page; Pushmeet Kohli and Swarat Chaudhuri) says human-guided agents specified Signal's security goals and proved the implementation meets them; every proof is checked in Lean. Official claims: first AI formal verification of a widely deployed cryptographic protocol "at this depth," and first implementation-level verification of such a protocol "by any method, with or without AI." About **four weeks**, vs years historically. Two previously fixed bugs, re-inserted into an isolated copy, produced machine-checked proofs of insecurity. Signal CTO Ehren Kret is quoted.
**Limits:** Only the specified properties, not an end-to-end bug-free claim. Distinct from DeepMind's RSA-1024 work (weekend-gap background).
**Action:** Treat as a lab milestone, not a reason to skip ordinary client patching.

## 4) GPUStack auth bypass CVE-2026-102895 added to KEV; due Nov 12 (KEV; exploited)
**What changed:** CISA added CVE-**2026-102895** in catalog **v2026.11.09** (Mon 2:54 PM EST; 1,773 entries, +4), due **Thu Nov 12**. The July 31 GHSA (CVSS 9.8) says GPUStack before **2.2.0** promotes a request to the built-in administrator when the source looks like loopback — via a same-host reverse proxy or a forged `X-GPUStack-Real-IP: 127.0.0.1` header — yielding unauthenticated admin and RCE (`POST /v2/inference-backends/from-yaml`). "Observed exploited in the wild against a v2.1.1 deployment."
**Limits:** Fix available since 2.2.0 (July). KEV add is the in-window event. Ransomware use: Unknown.
**Action:** Upgrade to 2.2.0+ before Nov 12, or set `force_auth_localhost=true` and strip the forwarding header.

## 5) OpenSSH "sshole" CVE-2026-123158 is now on KEV, due Nov 12 (KEV; exploited)
**What changed:** The same KEV drop adds CVE-**2026-123158** (due **Nov 12**). Ars (Mon 2:41 PM EST) reports GreyNoise saw **GT10** conduct "mature intrusions" on Southeast Asia honeypots, and that a backdoor "appears to be generated with the use of AI" — GreyNoise via Ars. Scope remains musl / non-PAM Linux; fix is OpenSSH 10.6p2. Zyxel CVE-2026-107441 is still not on KEV.
**Limits:** The AI wording is GreyNoise's, via Ars. Ransomware use: Unknown.
**Action:** Patch musl/non-PAM sshd to 10.6p2 and restart sshd before Nov 12.
