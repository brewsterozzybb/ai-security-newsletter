## 1) CyberStrikeAI runs post-exploitation in live intrusions against education orgs (AI-native; vendor IR)

**What changed:** [Kaspersky](https://securelist.com/tr/cyberstrikeai-attacks/121715/) (Mon 2:45 AM) says confirmed victims were "organizations in the education sector in several countries across Africa and Latin America," and that CyberStrikeAI "played a key role in the post-exploitation phase." After manual access and a two-week pause, the agent established persistence and local recon "within 2 minutes," ran domain recon over 5 hours, then lateral movement, credential dumping and database export "taking no more than one hour." The implant has "no API keys, no calls to AI service APIs"; the LLM sits on the attacker's C2. Separate from Oct 28's TrendAI item.

**Limits:** single vendor; victim count not given; attribution only that the tool "was developed within the Chinese cybersecurity community."

**Action:** alert on shells spawned by sqlservr.exe, burst-timed domain recon and machine-generated LDAP/SQL. The [tool is public](https://github.com/AIPentest/CyberStrikeAI).

## 2) More AI dev platforms report secret exposure: Trigger.dev env vars accessed; Northflank says control-plane secrets were the target (AI supply chain; vendor statements)

**What changed:** [Trigger.dev](https://trigger.dev/blog/security-incident-nov-2026) (Sun 5:33 PM): "The attacker gained unauthorized access to environment variables for these organizations," a "subset"; the exploit is blocked and owners were emailed. [Northflank](https://status.northflank.com/incident/cmvv0rn5a092f14rutwccodpp) (update Mon 6:09 AM) says the vector "was contained within an hour of the first alert," evidence "suggests it was focused on secrets held in our control plane," and "Some customers have reported unauthorised use of AI secrets" (first reported Oct 30, gap). Background: [Wiz](https://www.wiz.io/blog/black-october-for-ai-developer-platforms) (Fri, gap) lists nine AI developer platforms hit in October.

**Limits:** no root cause, vector or count from either vendor. No vendor links Trigger.dev to the campaign. A shared Linux kernel CVE (CVE-2026-98432) is a community claim only; Northflank calls its patch "separate."

**Action:** rotate every secret stored in Trigger.dev or Northflank, AI provider keys first; check provider usage logs.

## 3) Prefab UI sandbox escape makes FastMCP's generative-UI tool an RCE path, CVE-2026-120338 (CVE main 1 of 3; AI-native; CVSS 10; not exploited)

**What changed:** [NVD](https://nvd.nist.gov/vuln/detail/CVE-2026-120338) (Sun 11:18 AM; [GHSA](https://github.com/advisories/GHSA-3g72-rrqc-f76c) Sun 1:30 PM, CVSS 10). prefab-ui 0.14.0–0.20.2 runs "LLM-written UI" Python in a Deno sandbox with unrestricted file and network permissions, enabling file write and persistence. "The usual remote path is FastMCP's GenerativeUI provider… On a FastMCP server with no authentication configured, any client who can reach the server can call the tool." Fixed in 0.21.0 ([PR 538](https://github.com/PrefectHQ/prefab/pull/538)), released Oct 26.

**Limits:** opt-in feature; needs Deno. No exploitation reported; not on KEV. **Action:** upgrade to prefab-ui ≥0.21.0; require auth on FastMCP servers.

## 4) Ivanti Connect Secure: two more exploited zero-days complete the October chain (CVE/KEV main 2 of 3; exploited)

**What changed:** [Rapid7](https://www.rapid7.com/blog/post/etr-additional-ivanti-connect-secure-zero-days-exploited-in-the-wild-cve-2026-111357-cve-2026-118527/) (Mon 6:06 AM) covers CVE-2026-111357 (CVSS 8.8, local authenticated privilege escalation) and CVE-2026-118527 (4.3, file upload to a known path). Per Ivanti, "exploitation in the wild has already occurred prior to disclosure, affecting a limited number of customers when these flaws are chained with CVE-2026-104935," the October auth bypass. "Fixes are now available for all three." [KEV](https://www.cisa.gov/known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog) (v2026.10.30, Fri, gap) set -111357 **due today** and -118527 due Nov 13. Also [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/ivanti-patches-two-more-zero-days-exploited-in-connect-secure-attack-chain/) Mon 2:57 AM.

**Missed Oct 30** (Ivanti updated its advisory Oct 29, per Rapid7; no time given; advisory not read). KEV add Fri is in the weekend gap; in-window sources are Rapid7 and SecurityWeek. **Limits:** exploitation details come only from Ivanti as quoted by Rapid7. **Action:** clients who patched only CVE-2026-104935 in October are still exposed to the escalation step; patch all three and run integrity checks.

## 5) Second wave of supermarket freezer hacks across Europe (general cyber; consulting emergency; not AI)

**What changed:** [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/cyber-crime/2026/11/02/frozen-part-ii-hackers-remotely-defrost-produce-in-euro-supermarkets-again/5306924) (Mon 8:15 AM) reports "around 40 Delhaize stores" in Belgium hit overnight. [The Record](https://therecord.media/cyberattack-grocery-freezers-europe) (page stamp Mon 8:34 AM, just after window; event in window) says refrigerators "appear to have been hacked in the early hours of Monday morning," with "more than 50" stores in Belgium and "over a dozen" in Ireland, Estonia, the Netherlands and Luxembourg; freezers were set to 50°C. Installer Sabcobel said affected units "needed physical modifications and not just a software update." Danfoss and Copeland gear was named; October's French wave had ~700 victims (Paris prosecutor).

**Limits:** "suspected"; no claim or attribution. Belgium's CCB "did not yet know whether AI was used." **Action:** for retail and cold-chain clients, find internet-exposed refrigeration controllers and plan for on-site fixes.
