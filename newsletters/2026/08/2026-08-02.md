### EU AI Act Main Phase Enforcement Begins Today, August 2, 2026 — Brussels Gains Sweeping Powers Over the World's Largest AI Models, With Fines Up to €15M or 3% of Global Revenue

The Commission can **request technical documentation, access models, evaluate them, require corrective measures, impose fines of up to €15 million or 3% of global annual revenue** (whichever is higher), and, per Euractiv, "go as far as blocking access to the EU market" if a developer has not complied with obligations to evaluate and mitigate risks. The Parliament Magazine frames the moment as the test of whether the Commission is "willing, and equipped" to use the new powers; **Henna Virkkunen, the EU's Commissioner for Digital and Frontier Technologies**, said Friday that "as enforcement begins, we are taking an important step towards AI that people and businesses can understand and trust, and whose benefits are shared widely across our society."
